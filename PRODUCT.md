# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

International B2B textile & apparel **sourcing buyers, merchandisers, and compliance officers** evaluating EMIGARMENTS as a manufacturing partner across global markets. They arrive skeptical, doing desk research and comparing several suppliers at once. In under ~60 seconds they are scanning for four answers:

1. Is this a *real, modern factory* — not a trading-house front or a directory listing?
2. What can they actually make? (Leather, Denim, Home Textiles, Towels & Terry.)
3. Are they credible and compliant? (Certifications, capacity, process control.)
4. How do I start an inquiry / get a quote?

They often forward the page to colleagues, so it must read as serious and self-evidently legitimate on first glance.

## Product Purpose

A **credibility-first marketing site** (not e-commerce) that converts skeptical international buyers into qualified inquiries. It establishes EMIGARMENTS as a **progressive, vertically-integrated industrial group** spanning four divisions — modern automated facilities, precision manufacturing, audited quality, responsible production. The whole site is a funnel toward one action: a low-friction **inquiry / quote request** (plus a downloadable company profile).

Success = qualified RFQ/inquiry submissions and company-profile downloads from serious buyers. Failure = looking like every other AI-generated supplier page, or making vague quality claims a buyer can't verify.

## Brand Personality

**Precise · Industrial · Premium.** The voice is confident, exact, and substantial — it speaks in concrete capability and proof, never adjectives or hype. EMIGARMENTS was founded in **2021 in Karachi, Pakistan** (founder **Muhammad Musa**); newness is framed as an *advantage* — modern automation, current standards, responsible manufacturing — never as inexperience, and never disguised as fake heritage. Emotional goal: a sourcing buyer should feel *"this is a serious, capable, modern factory I can put my brand's name behind."*

## Anti-references

- **NOT a stuffy corporate PDF / annual report** — no dense lifeless text walls, no dated "company profile brochure" stiffness.
- **NOT flashy consumer / DTC fashion** — this sells to businesses, not shoppers; no trendy, loud, lifestyle-influencer energy.
- **NOT a cheap supplier-directory / Alibaba listing** — no cluttered, low-trust, stock-soup look.
- **NOT the editorial-magazine reflex** (display-serif + italic + drop-caps + ruled broadsheet columns) — impeccable's currently-saturated brand lane; we are an industrial group, not a literary magazine.

## Design Principles

1. **Proof over adjectives.** Every quality claim is backed by a concrete number, a named process, or a real certification — never "high quality" standing alone. Numbers and named capabilities beat marketing language for B2B trust.
2. **Modern, not heritage.** Founded 2021 is an asset: newer facilities, current automation, responsible manufacturing. Never borrow the "decades of legacy" language the reference sites lean on — it would read as dishonest to compliance buyers.
3. **The material is the hero.** Indigo denim, leather grain, terry pile, woven cloth — the textiles themselves carry the brand's warmth and credibility. Show real material and real facility; never empty minimalism, never generic stock-soup.
4. **One confident voice — commit to it.** A disciplined indigo-navy world with a single camel accent; no hedging, no decoration for its own sake. Distinctive beats safe; safe is invisible.
5. **Every page ends in an easy inquiry.** The site is one funnel to a low-friction quote/inquiry; the next step to contact is never more than a scroll away.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target **WCAG 2.2 AA**. Body text ≥ 4.5:1 contrast (the camel accent is for large text, rules, and ornament only — never body copy on light). Full keyboard operability with visible focus states, semantic landmarks/headings, and descriptive alt text written in the brand voice. Every animation has a `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` alternative (crossfade or instant). Mobile-first responsive with ≥44px touch targets; copy and headings tested for overflow at every breakpoint. English at launch, but copy is kept in structured data so a future Russian/Urdu localization doesn't require a rebuild.
